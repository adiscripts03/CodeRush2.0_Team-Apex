# ADR-005: Human Approval Governance & Immutable Audit Trail

## Context

Emergency Operations Center (EOC) decisions involve life-safety risks and resource allocation. Allowing autonomous AI systems to execute actions without human verification creates severe operational and legal risks. Furthermore, lack of auditability prevents post-disaster learning and accountability.

We needed to decide how to govern recommendation execution and audit logging.

## Decision

We decided to implement:
1. **Mandatory Human Approval Gate**: All recommendations generated by the Agentic Decision Planner are created with `status: "proposed"`. Execution occurs ONLY after explicit human approval via `POST /approvals/approve` or `POST /approvals/:id/approve`.
2. **Mandatory Rejection Rationale**: Any rejection via `POST /approvals/reject` MUST include a non-empty `rejectionReason`.
3. **Automated Action Execution Side-Effects**: Approving a recommendation automatically updates the underlying simulation state (e.g. opening shelters, deploying boats) and transitions status to `executed`.
4. **Immutable Audit Event Stream**: All actions, approvals, rejections, and execution results emit structured events to the `audit_events` collection.

## Consequences

- **Pros**:
  - Guarantees strict human-in-the-loop oversight.
  - Ensures complete accountability and traceability for every decision.
  - Automates complex multi-system simulation state updates upon approval.
- **Cons**:
  - Requires human operator presence to process pending recommendation queues.

## Status

Accepted and implemented in Milestone 8.
